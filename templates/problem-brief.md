# Problem brief

One page, filled in before anyone opens a design tool. If a box is hard to fill, that's the finding.

## The question
Write the request as it was given, then rewrite it as the real question.

- As given: *"Redesign the dashboard."*
- The real question: *"Why are people opening the dashboard and still calling support?"*

## 1. What behavior are we trying to change?
Who does what today, and what should they do instead?

## 2. What evidence tells us this is the real problem?
Heatmaps, analytics, support tickets, interviews, test results. Link them. If the honest answer is "a hunch", say so and plan how to check it.

## 3. What happens if we do nothing?
The cost of leaving it alone, in time, money, trust or risk.

## Whose problem matters most?
If there are several groups (visitors, customers, the support team, compliance), rank them for this project.

## What success changes
The one or two numbers or behaviors that should move, and roughly by how much.

## What we are not designing
The tempting things that are out of scope this time.

## Constraints
Legal, compliance, accessibility, technical, timeline. In regulated products, list consent and disclosure needs here, not at the end.
