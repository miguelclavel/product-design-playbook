# The short version

A recruiter gives a case study about 30 seconds before deciding whether to keep reading. So put the whole project at the top, before any images: three parts, two lines each, plain language.

## Template

**The problem**
Two lines. What was wrong, and for whom.

**What I did**
Two lines. Your part, in verbs. Not the team's whole roadmap.

**What changed**
Two lines. The result you can stand behind. If it wasn't measured, say what you'd measure.

## Example

**The problem**
A car insurance quote asked people 30 questions before giving them anything, in one long form filled in by typing.

**What I did**
Rebuilt it as a conversation, one clear question at a time, with every answer editable and consent asked at the point it's needed.

**What changed**
23 form fields became 19 questions across 5 named stages, answered by tapping instead of typing.
