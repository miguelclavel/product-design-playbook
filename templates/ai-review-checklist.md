# AI review checklist

For anything AI helped make (a research summary, a draft, copy, a prototype, code) before it reaches a stakeholder or a user. AI failures are rarely loud. The dangerous ones are quiet.

## Numbers
- [ ] Every statistic is traced back to its source
- [ ] Percentages, dates and counts match the source exactly, not "about right"
- [ ] Nothing was rounded into a better story

## Specifics
- [ ] Every feature name, product, person, date and reference actually exists
- [ ] Quotes are real and attributed correctly
- [ ] The more specific a claim is, the harder it was checked

## Tone
- [ ] The emotional register fits the subject (money, health, layoffs and errors are not cheerful)
- [ ] It sounds like us, not like a template

## What's missing
- [ ] What risk, edge case or group of people did it not mention?
- [ ] What would a skeptic in the room ask first?

## For AI in the product itself
- [ ] People can see what the system did and step in (human oversight)
- [ ] The system suggests; people decide (user control)
- [ ] It says what it's doing and why (transparency)
- [ ] Every answer can be edited, undone or taken back a step (correction and recovery)
- [ ] The same input leads to the same kind of result (predictable outcomes)
- [ ] It's obvious what the system does and doesn't do (clear boundaries)

Trust the speed. Audit the substance. Both, always.
