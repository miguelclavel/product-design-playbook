# Experiment plan

Written before the test starts, so the result can't be argued into whatever anyone hoped.

## Hypothesis
Because we saw **[evidence]**, we believe that **[change]** will cause **[behavior]** for **[audience]**.

## What changes
- Control:
- Variant:

Change one thing, or be clear that you're testing a whole direction.

## The number that decides it
One primary metric, for example click through rate, quote completion or leads.

## Guardrails
Numbers that must not get worse while the main one moves (errors, drop offs, complaints, revenue per visit).

## Audience and traffic
Who sees it, what share of traffic, which devices.

## How long, and how big
Minimum run time (at least one full week, so weekdays and weekends both count) and the sample needed to trust the result.

## Decision rule
What we do if it wins, loses, or is flat. Decide this now.

## Result
Filled in after: what happened, the numbers, and what we learned.

## Share it
Add the result to the monthly testing review, wins and losses alike. A flat test that everyone hears about is worth more than a win nobody remembers.
